# HGW — Approved scope and USD 1 billion aggregate programme target
**Project instruction / design baseline — 28 October 2026**
**DRAFT ONLY — NO OFFER, GUARANTEE, INDEPENDENT VALUATION, TOKEN MINT, OR ISSUANCE**

## 1. Nine approved activity sectors
1. Food / الغذاء — marketplace access and supply programmes.
2. Energy / الطاقة — generation, storage and energy-related services, subject to licences.
3. Telecommunications / الاتصالات — connectivity and communications services, subject to sector rules.
4. Transport / المواصلات — mobility and transport services.
5. Investment / الاستثمار — **separately regulated** access to investment information or eligible services; no automatic right to profits or shares from utility holdings.
6. Education / التعليم — learning programmes and training.
7. Health / الصحة — eligible health and wellbeing services subject to clinical and privacy rules.
8. Innovation / الابتكار — research, IP-related services and technological development.
9. Welfare / الرفاهية — human wellbeing, housing and other permissible support services.

These are **project objectives and proposed service categories**. They are not assertions that services are currently operating, funded, licensed, available for redemption or supplied by a duly authorised token issuer. Rights to specific offerings require issuer identification and binding product terms, availability, fees, consumer safeguards and any sector-specific permission.

## 2. Programme financial target
**USD 1,000,000,000 — aggregate planned programme size across tokens and financial instruments, taken as a user-approved planning target.**

This is **not** any of:
- legally issued or paid-up capital;
- independent appraisal or enterprise valuation;
- value of current assets/collateral or financial reserves;
- investor funds raised or investment commitments;
- guaranteed token price, asset parity or minimum redemption value;
- aggregate market capitalisation or liquid cash.

Instruments must have separately approved issuance quantities, pricing, rights, issuer identity and denomination. Never sum an underlying asset and a token claim representing that same asset twice (avoid double counting). Likewise, converting a portfolio balance into HVU does not create additional financial value. Currency conversion to SEK/EUR depends on real transaction date and verifiable FX evidence; no fixed FX rate is adopted.

## 3. No implied unity of legal claims
HGW-U utility: contractually specified access to **issuer-provided** services only, where defined and actually available; no automatic shareholder or profit claim.
HGW-G governance: governance voting rights only under enforceable governance rules; not statutory shareholder voting.
HGW-E equity: shareholder rights only after legal AB incorporation, formal authorised issue, share-register entries and applicable approvals.
HGW-S, GS, SS sukuk: distinct series-level rights under binding contracts, assets, profits/loss waterfall and applicable law; green and smart labels do not create a fixed guaranteed return.
RWA/IP: legally evidenced property or license rights, not merely token possession.

## 4. Allocation remains undecided
No percentages or USD allocations across the nine sectors or instrument families were supplied. None are invented. The USD 1bn should remain an **unallocated aggregate ceiling/target for programme design** until an issuer-by-issuer financing model, double-counting reconciliation, cap table, use-of-proceeds and independently verified pricing have been approved.

## 5. Pre-issuance controls
The following remain off: live sale, mint, bridge, investment subscription, securities distribution and fundraising. Before changing this: eligible legal-person issuer; definitive MiCA/MiFID classification; final prospectus/white paper and notification or approval/exemption as appropriate; complete iXBRL where required; audited contracts; payments and banking onboarding; AML/KYC; authority to sign; and explicit final approval.

**Approved only as project design and planning targets. Not a regulatory filing or securities offering.**
