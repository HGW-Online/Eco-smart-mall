## Explanation required by Article 8(4) — UNAPPROVED CLASSIFICATION MEMO
### I. Is this excluded under Article 2(4) / MiFID financial instrument?
Preliminary design of HGW-U excludes rights to shares, dividends, project finance proceeds, debt claims and property title. It is proposed as an access credential to issuer-supplied goods/services; no final service agreement exists. Transferability, actual token functionality and contractual terms are still unresolved. **No conclusive exclusion determination is possible** without final terms, instrument governance, transferability and counsel review.

### II. Is it an EMT?
HGW-U is **not intended** to maintain stable value by referencing **one official currency**, and no 1:1 fiat redemption has been designed. The internal HVU accounting comparator must not be offered or presented as fiat-pegged currency. Actual pricing, marketing and redemption promises must be reviewed before a defensible conclusion.

### III. Is it an ART?
HGW-U is **not intended** to maintain stable value by reference to other values, assets, rights or currency baskets. The project envisions consolidated portfolio reporting of independent assets, not collateral backing or a price peg. If HGW-U is marketed as sharing a fixed value with shares/sukuk/assets, **ART classification risk is material** and the proposal must be redesigned or separately authorised.

### IV. Is it a utility token or another OTHR crypto-asset?
Access to issuer-provided actual goods or services is the target, but multi-merchant marketplace payments, unfinished services and speculative marketing may change the assessment. A signed issuer-service access contract, final token architecture and counsel-backed MiCA classification test are mandatory.

### V. Is an exemption under MiCA Article 4 available?
No exemption is asserted. Consider Article 4(2) thresholds and investor-only conditions, Article 4(3) free distribution/existing operating service/limited network only with documentary evidence, Article 4(4) admission-to-trading consequences and any relevant time-limits and conditions. **Do not infer exemption merely because the proposed issuer is a sole proprietor**.
