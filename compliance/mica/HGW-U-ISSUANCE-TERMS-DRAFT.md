Date: 2026-10-28. Instrument: HGW-U (working name). Intended category: issuer-service access token, contingent on counsel's MiCA classification.

## Design defaults for review — NOT APPROVED
- Maximum contemplated supply: **1,000,000,000** units, across all chains combined, *not per chain*. Do not interpret this figure as already issued or irrevocably fixed.
- Primary chains for test: Arbitrum Sepolia and Solana Devnet; mainnet only after separate audits, legally authorised issuer and final contracts.
- Decimals, issuance, mint/burn mechanism, treasury allotment, reserves, team lockups, community rewards, liquidity, price, fees, vesting, subscription sizes, minimum/maximum allocations and sale dates: **UNDECIDED**.
- Functional rights: access only to specifically named services supplied by an eligible issuer once binding service terms exist. Third-party marketplace payments require distinct review.
- No shareholding, dividend, coupon, interest, voting in company meetings, sukuk claim, collateral, fixed redemption or peg to HVU.
- Transfers may be blocked in jurisdictions, situations or wallets where legally required. Nothing guarantees listing, liquidity or cross-chain interoperability.
- No payments or purchase instructions before final legally compliant offer and technical clearance.

## Draft user terms
1. The contract must identify the actual provider and eligible purchaser jurisdiction.
2. The exact service, deliverable, availability, access unit, expiry and cancellation rules will be specified before any offer.
3. Personal data handled by privacy notice; no raw biometric identifier on public blockchain.
4. No refund promise outside contract and non-waivable consumer law.
5. Disputes, complaints, governing law, venue and contact must be filled and checked.
6. Technical risk includes permanent loss from address mistakes, compromised keys and chain/bridge failure.
7. All advertised unit prices and rights require consistent published disclosures.
