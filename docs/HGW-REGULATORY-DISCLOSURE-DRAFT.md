### MiCA applicability and exemption decision log
Under Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114, a public offer of a crypto-asset **other than** ART/EMT generally requires legal-person offeror, compliant white paper, notification, publication and other duties, subject to precise exceptions. Examples:
- Article 4(2): fewer than 150 persons **per Member State** acting for themselves; aggregate consideration at most **EUR 1 million in 12 months** across the Union; solely qualified investors where token ownership is restricted accordingly. These generally remove certain white-paper/notification/publication requirements, **not every obligation**.
- Article 4(3): certain genuinely free distributions, validation rewards, utility token for an **existing/operating** good/service, or limited contractual merchant network. The limited-network case has a special notification if consideration exceeds EUR 1 million per applicable 12-month period.
- Exceptions in (2) and (3) cannot be relied upon where the issuer/agent announces intended admission to trading as specified by Article 4(4). “Utility” as a label is insufficient. If an unbuilt service is involved, Article 4(6) limits the offer duration as specified.
- If token is an **asset-referenced token (ART)** or **e-money token (EMT)**, a different, stricter regime applies; do not launch under this pack.
- If it is a **financial instrument**, MiCA does not replace securities law.
A self-declared exemption is not a regulatory confirmation. Decisions and evidence must be documented by qualified Swedish/EU counsel.

## 7. Exchange / multi-chain settlement — restrictions
The app may show consolidated portfolio positions without granting universal interchangeability. A canonical record must map verified issuer obligations and authorised rights to unique immutable identifiers. At most one valid representation of the same claim may be circulating per legal entitlement (with fully reconciled escrow/cancel/mint controls). Cross-chain movement for *non-financial* tokens requires onchain audits and reliable bridging. Financial instruments need qualified register/custody rules and transfer approvals; users cannot exchange 1 equity token into 1 sukuk based solely on a common HVU report.
